06 — My weakest asset

What I'd change, and why.

BlueMagic asset four closes in second person about the viewer's condition: If you're dealing with a receding hairline or thinning…

Meta's health enforcement went claims-based in July 2026. Before and after imagery is fine now. What still is not fine is copy implying you know a viewer's health status, and enforcement now catches the indirect version.

The other three stay in first-person testimonial from open to close, which is the correct construction and carries the same weight. So the fix costs nothing.

The wider lesson: on a restricted vertical, the compliance read belongs in the scripting pass. Once it is rendered, a policy rewrite is a reshoot.
