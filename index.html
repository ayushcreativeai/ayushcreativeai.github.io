02 · Meta · UK and EU

An Istanbul hair-transplant clinic selling a home protocol into one of the most expensive lead-gen categories on Meta.

Broad beat every custom audience by eight times. The winning creative beat its sibling by four.

12.8%CTR, winning ad
Dating stakes

“I matched with her three days ago. I still haven't replied. Because of my hair.”

A live, unresolved situation and its cause, inside three seconds. Best hook on the account.

Social avoidance

“I used to avoid every photo, every mirror, every event.”

Inventory of small refusals, then a month one, three, six arc.

Too late

“I was losing the one thing I couldn't buy back.”

For the buyer who already tried everything and stopped believing.

Result first

“Six months ago I thought I was going bald. This is my hair now.”

Payoff up front. Also the weakest of the four, and the teardown below says why.

05 · Statics · 1080 × 1350

Six levers the video could not pull

A presenter can only test what a presenter can say. Every line here went through the platform policy checklist before layout, not after rejection.

Echo · the manufacturing problem

So I stopped making videos, and built the line that makes them.

The problem

Thirty five videos, a day of work in each. Hand production does not scale here, it just gets more expensive every week.

What I did first

A full content audit before a single frame. I scored 182 of the account's own reels by reach and posting hour.

The solution

One command takes a script to a scheduled post, gated by a transcript check and an approval that times out in an hour.
